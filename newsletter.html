A note from your neighborhood

Welcome to The Estates Six

There is something distinctive about living in Camelback Country Estates: familiar streets, mature desert landscaping, and the sense of place embodied by our entrance on Doubletree Ranch Road. This monthly letter is designed to reflect that character—and to make it easier for neighbors to know what matters around us.

Each issue will focus first on information that can affect life in Paradise Valley, then take a carefully edited look at worthwhile events and discoveries in Scottsdale and Phoenix. We will also make room for news, recommendations and photographs shared by residents themselves.

This is a sample issue for Angie's review, not a notice of HOA action. Association announcements and resident content will appear only after Angie has reviewed and approved them.

01 / Paradise Valley watch

Town matters worth keeping an eye on

Follow Town Council decisions—not just headlines

Paradise Valley's Town Council generally meets on the second and fourth Thursdays, with a reduced schedule in December. The town maintains a public calendar with agendas, packets, action summaries and meeting video when available. It's a useful place to follow proposed land-use changes, planning questions and matters that may affect neighboring streets.

Neighbor takeaway: Before hearing about a project secondhand, open the actual agenda packet and distinguish an item under discussion from a final decision. Town meeting calendar →

Planning and property questions

For any proposed work in or around the neighborhood, the agenda and supporting documents are a better starting point than social media. We will flag relevant hearings and explain practical implications—but only when the facts and location are verified.

Source: Town agendas

One small rule that prevents an eyesore

Paradise Valley says trash containers may go to the curb the night before collection and must be put away in a screened area by the end of collection day. Collection itself is handled by private providers, not the Town.

Source: PV trash and recycling guidance

Town meetings and proposed ordinances • land use and construction activity • road work and public safety • relevant utilities and homeowner guidance. HOA-specific notices will be a separate section reviewed by Angie.

03 / Useful corner

A few practical things to know

Holiday guest plans?

Send visiting friends the official event pages before they arrive. Many December favorites run on selected nights only, and reservations or tickets may be necessary.

Heading into Phoenix on Dec. 5?

The Electric Light Parade can make an evening route around Camelback Road and Central Avenue inconvenient. Check the published city closure map before leaving.

Keeping the neighborhood looking its best

The Town of Paradise Valley's trash guidance includes curb-placement and storage expectations. Your service schedule comes from your private collection provider; holiday adjustments should be confirmed directly with that company.
